Getting started

Three steps. Once you sign in, the dashboard walks you through each one.

  1. Sign in

    Create one login for your agency, or sign in to the one you have.

    Sign up

  2. Connect the Claude plugin

    Install the Roster plugin in the Claude desktop app, connect it to your account, and run /roster:setup. The dashboard shows you how, step by step.

  3. Start creating reports and campaigns

    Add a client, connect its Google Ads or Meta, then ask Claude for a report or a new campaign. You see the figures, or every ad, before anything is saved, and campaigns are created paused.

    Meta Ads, during early access: Meta asks us to invite you as a tester before your ad accounts can connect. Ask for the invite and accept it once.

Stuck? Write to us and you'll hear back from a person.
