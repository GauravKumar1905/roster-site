Getting started

Roster is hosted. There is nothing to install, no server to run, and no folder to create on your machine — including for the Claude part. If a step below sounds like setup, it is setup of your clients, not of software.

1. Create your account

One account for your agency, shared by whoever does the reporting. Sign up here.

There is no per-seat charge and no invitation flow: the people who need it use the same login. If you need to know which individual did what, Roster does not currently record that, and you should not rely on it for that purpose.

2. Add your first client

A workspace is one client. Name it after them — “Northside Coffee”, not “Google Ads” — because everything else hangs off that name: the connection, the accounts, the reports, and the links you send.

Make one workspace per client rather than one big one. Separation is the point: a report lives inside a workspace and cannot see another workspace's data.

3. Connect Google Ads

Inside the workspace, choose Connect Google Ads and sign in with the Google account that can reach that client's advertising. That is usually your agency's account, not the client's own login.

Roster only reads. It never creates, edits, pauses or deletes a campaign, a budget, a bid or a keyword. It cannot, and there is no setting that turns that on.

While Roster is in early access, Google expires this authorisation every seven days. You will need to reconnect each workspace weekly until that changes. It is Google's limit rather than ours, and it goes away once our consent screen is verified.

4. Choose the accounts

Roster asks Google what that login can reach and lists everything back — for an agency login that is often a hundred accounts or more, so there is a search box. Add the ones that belong to this client.

Manager accounts appear but cannot be added. An MCC holds other accounts rather than campaigns of its own, and Google refuses to report metrics on one. Add the client account underneath it instead.

5. Connect Claude

You design reports by asking for them. Two ways to set that up:

The plugin (recommended)

In Claude Code:

/plugin marketplace add GauravKumar1905/roster-plugin
/plugin install roster@roster
/roster:setup

That brings commands, an agent for building several reports at once, and the guidance Claude needs to design reports that hold up over time rather than ones shaped around this month's numbers.

Or the connector on its own

In Claude’s settings, add a custom connector pointing at: 

https://roster-1035727789436.asia-southeast1.run.app/api/mcp

Either way, the first time Claude uses Roster it opens a browser so you can sign in and approve the connection. That happens once. Claude sees only your agency's workspaces.

6. Ask for a report

Plain language is the interface:

Build a monthly performance report for Northside Coffee.

Claude inspects the account first, then designs around what the data actually supports — an account with no conversion tracking gets reach and efficiency rather than empty cost-per-acquisition charts. It saves the design and hands back a dashboard URL.

That link is what you send your client. It refreshes on its own from then on, and it keeps every past version, so it never goes blank while an update is running. Anyone with the link can open it without signing in, so treat it as you would treat the report itself.

What happens after

Nothing, which is the point. The report you designed once keeps producing current numbers without you, and without further cost. You come back when a client wants something different.

If something goes wrong

“This is a manager account”
You added an MCC. Add one of the client accounts underneath it instead.
A report shows fewer widgets than expected
Widgets needing data the account does not have are skipped rather than shown empty. The report page lists what was skipped and why.
“No Google connection”
The workspace's authorisation has expired — the seven-day limit above. Reconnect it in that workspace's settings.
Claude cannot see your clients
It is connected to a different Roster account, or not connected at all. Run /roster:setup, or re-approve the connector.
