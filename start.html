Getting started

Roster does four things through Claude: Google Ads reporting, Meta Ads reporting, Google Ads campaign creation and Meta Ads campaign creation. Reports work today; Meta Ads campaign creation is in early access, and Google Ads campaign creation opens soon. Your first report is three steps away.

  1. Create your login

    Create one login for your agency. Roster then opens on its setup page.

    Create account

  2. Set up Roster in Claude

    Copy the setup message from the setup page. In the Claude desktop app, open the Code tab, start a new session, paste the message and send. Claude installs the Roster plugin and connects it to your login. It stops when it needs you, for example to approve a command or to sign in, and the setup page lists each stop.

    When Claude says setup is recorded, go back to Roster and press Done. Roster then asks what you want to do first.

  3. Pick your first report

    Roster asks “What do you want to do first?” Pick Google Ads report or Meta Ads report. Roster asks for the sign-in there — once, for your agency, with the Google or Facebook login that manages your clients’ ads — and then for the client’s ad account. Add a note for Claude if you like, and press Copy for Claude.

    Paste the message into Claude and send. Claude shows you each page with real figures before saving it, and the report’s page in Roster follows along each time you come back to it: Waiting for Claude, then Claude is building it, then Ready to share.

    When it’s ready, press Keep as client: the name you keep is the title your client sees on their reports. Then press Share with client, and Create link: Roster copies the link, ready to paste into your email.

    Campaign creation is in early access. To turn it on for your agency, email gauravkr.contact@gmail.com. Once it’s on, a Meta Ads campaign starts the same way, in Roster: pick Meta Ads campaign, choose the client’s ad account — Roster checks what it can set up there — and describe the campaign in a line. Claude shows you every ad first, and Roster creates the campaign paused, only after your yes. Google Ads campaigns open soon.

    Meta Ads, during early access: Meta asks us to invite you as a tester before you can connect. Register as a Meta developer (it’s free). Then, on the Meta Ads report or in Settings, give the Facebook profile link or name you’ll connect with and press Ask for an invite. Accept the invite at developers.facebook.com/requests once it arrives, then connect.

Stuck? Write to us and you'll hear back from a person.
