Getting started

Roster does four things through Claude: Google Ads reporting, Meta Ads reporting, Google Ads campaign creation and Meta Ads campaign creation. Campaign creation opens soon. Reports work today. Your first report is three steps away.

  1. Create your login

    Create one login for your agency. Roster then opens on its setup page.

    Create account

  2. Set up Roster in Claude

    Copy the setup message from the setup page. In the Claude desktop app, open the Code tab, start a new session, paste the message and send. Claude installs the Roster plugin and connects it to your login. It stops when it needs you, for example to approve a command or to sign in, and the setup page lists each stop.

    When Claude says setup is recorded, go back to Roster and press Done.

  3. Connect, add a client, then ask Claude

    In Settings, connect Google Ads or Meta Ads once, signing in with the Google or Facebook login that manages your clients’ ads; it covers every client. Add your first client and pick the ad accounts that are theirs. Then ask Claude for a report, check the figures, and send your client one link.

    When campaign creation opens, ask Claude for a new campaign the same way. It shows you every ad first, and Roster creates the campaign paused, only after your yes.

    Meta Ads, during early access: Meta asks us to invite you as a tester before you can connect. Register as a Meta developer (it’s free), then ask for the invite for the Facebook account you’ll connect with, and accept it once.
