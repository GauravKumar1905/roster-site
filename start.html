Getting started

Three steps. Once you sign in, the dashboard walks you through each one.

  1. Sign in

    Create one login for your agency, or sign in to the one you have.

    Sign up

  2. Connect the Claude plugin

    Install the Roster plugin in the Claude desktop app, connect it to your account, and run /roster:setup. The dashboard shows you how, step by step.

  3. Start creating reports

    Add a client, connect its Google Ads, then ask Claude for a report. You see the figures before anything is saved, and the link you send your client refreshes itself.
