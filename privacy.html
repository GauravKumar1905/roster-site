2. What we collect

Account details
Your email address and a password. Passwords are never stored in readable form — we keep only a salted scrypt hash, and cannot recover your password.
Session cookies
One cookie that identifies your signed-in session, and a short-lived cookie used during Google sign-in to prevent request forgery. Both are httpOnly, so page scripts cannot read them. See section 7.
Workspace details
The client names you create, and which Google Ads accounts you attach to each. These are typed by you and may identify your clients.
Google Ads data
Account names and IDs, campaign names and IDs, and performance figures such as spend, impressions, clicks and conversions. Rendered reports are stored as dated snapshots so a report keeps its history and a client link does not go blank mid-refresh.
Google account identity
The email address of the Google account used to connect, shown so you can tell which login a workspace is using.

We do not collect payment card details, and we do not ask for or store credentials for any service other than the Google authorisation described below.

3. Google user data

When you connect a workspace, Google asks you to authorise Roster and then issues us a refresh token. We store that token encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM and use it for one purpose: reading the Google Ads data needed to render your reports.

Roster requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords scope, which Google classes as sensitive, plus your Google account email address. The adwords scope permits writes; Roster performs read operations only and never creates, edits, pauses or deletes anything in your advertising accounts.

Roster's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Specifically, we do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not transfer it except as described in section 5, and we do not allow humans to read it except where you have asked us to for support, where it is necessary for security or to comply with law, or where the data is aggregated and de-identified.

You can revoke Roster's access at any time from your Google account permissions page, or by disconnecting the workspace inside Roster. Disconnecting deletes the stored token and the list of accounts attached to that workspace.

4. Why we process it

  • To provide the service — signing you in, fetching your Google Ads figures and rendering reports. This is necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • To keep the service secure — session handling, abuse prevention and audit. This is our legitimate interest.
  • To understand how the product is used — aggregate analytics, as described in section 7.
  • To meet legal obligations — where we are required to retain or disclose information.

We do not use your data to train machine learning models, and we do not sell it.

6. Shared report links

Report links of the form /r/<report-id> are deliberately viewable without signing in, so you can send them to a client. The identifier is long and random, and we do not list or index these pages — but anyone who has the link can see that report. Treat a report link as you would treat the report itself, and disconnect or delete a report if a link should stop working.

7. Analytics and cookies

We measure how the product is used, and we do it without identifying anyone. Google Analytics runs here in a cookieless configuration: storage consent is permanently denied, so it sets no _ga cookie, keeps no persistent identifier, and cannot recognise you across visits or across devices.

That is why you are not asked to accept cookies for analytics — there is no analytics cookie to accept. It is also a deliberate trade: we can see that a page was opened and roughly how long it held attention, and we cannot see that it was you, or connect today's visit to last week's.

What is recorded:

  • which page or screen was opened;
  • approximately how many seconds a page or a section of a page was visible;
  • product events such as a workspace being created or accounts being discovered, and a count where a count is meaningful;
  • coarse technical context supplied by any browser request — device type, browser, language, and an approximate location derived from the IP address, which Google discards rather than storing.

What is never sent:

  • your name or email address;
  • your clients' names, or any workspace name you have typed;
  • Google Ads account IDs, campaign names, or any advertising figure;
  • anything that could be used to single out one person.

We do not use advertising cookies, we do not run cross-site tracking, and we do not sell anything to anyone. The cookies Roster does set are the two described in section 2 — your sign-in session and the short-lived one used during Google sign-in. Both are strictly necessary to log in at all, and neither is used for measurement. If you would still rather opt out of Google Analytics everywhere, Google publishes a browser add-on for it.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account and workspace records — until you ask us to delete them.
  • Google refresh tokens — until you disconnect the workspace or revoke access at Google, whichever comes first.
  • Report snapshots — retained as history so past reports remain viewable. Deleted with the report or the workspace.
  • Sessions — expire automatically, and are destroyed when you sign out.
  • Analytics — per the retention period configured in Google Analytics. Because the data holds no identifier, it cannot be traced back to you in any case.

Deleting a workspace deletes its connection, its attached accounts, its reports and their snapshots.

9. Security

  • Google refresh tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM.
  • Passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes and are not recoverable.
  • Traffic is served over HTTPS.
  • Session cookies are httpOnly and sameSite, and sessions are revoked server-side on sign-out rather than left to expire.
  • Each workspace's data is scoped to its owning account in the database query itself, so a mistaken identifier returns nothing rather than another agency's records.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe you have found a vulnerability, please write to gauravkr.contact@gmail.com before disclosing it publicly.

One practical note: a single Roster login may be shared by several people at your agency, so we cannot always tell which individual performed an action. If you need per-person accountability, ask us and we will tell you what is and is not attributable.
