Roster vs Looker Studio for Google Ads client reporting

Updated 1 November 2026 · written by the Roster team

Looker Studio is free, flexible and combines many data sources, but you build each client's report yourself and keep it working as campaigns change.

Roster builds a Google Ads report from a plain description, checks the figures against the account, and refreshes it each month, but it covers Google Ads only and needs the Claude desktop app. Pick Looker Studio for multi-platform dashboards; pick Roster to stop rebuilding monthly Google Ads reports.

Where Looker Studio is the better choice

  • Reports that combine platforms. If a client's report must show Google Ads next to Meta, GA4 and their CRM, Looker Studio handles it and Roster doesn't yet.
  • Full control of the design. Custom layouts, brand colours, blended data and calculated fields are all there.
  • Live dashboards. For a client who wants to check numbers any day of the month, a live dashboard suits better than a monthly report.
  • No new tools. Everyone already has a Google account; Roster asks you to use the Claude desktop app.

Where Roster is the better choice

  • You rebuild the same Google Ads report every month. Roster's report refreshes from live data; you add the commentary and send.
  • You don't want to build dashboards. You describe what the client should see, and Roster works out the charts from what the account actually supports.
  • Figures must match the account. Every rate, such as cost per conversion and CTR, is worked out from totals the way Google Ads does it.
  • The client should see what you checked. Each link you send keeps the figures as they were when you sent it, so last month's report never changes under the client.

Questions

Is Looker Studio free for Google Ads reporting?
Yes. Looker Studio is free, and Google's own Google Ads connector is free. You pay only if you add paid connectors for other platforms or choose Looker Studio Pro.
Can I use Roster and Looker Studio together?
Yes. Some agencies keep a Looker Studio dashboard for their own day-to-day monitoring and send clients a Roster report each month. Roster only reads Google Ads, so the two don't interfere.
Does Roster cover Meta, LinkedIn or GA4?
Not yet. Roster covers Google Ads only. If a client's report must combine several platforms, Looker Studio or a multi-source reporting tool fits better today.
