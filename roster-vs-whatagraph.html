Roster vs Whatagraph for freelancers and small agencies

Checked against whatagraph.com on 2 November 2026 · written by the Roster team

Whatagraph is a full marketing data platform for agencies and brands: more than 60 data sources, dashboards, scheduled PDF reports and AI agents, from €699 a month billed yearly, bought through a demo call.

Roster does less, for less: Google Ads and Meta Ads only, client reports and campaign setup done by asking Claude, free during early access, and you can sign up yourself today.

Where Whatagraph is the better choice

  • You report on far more than ads. SEO, email, CRM and ecommerce data in the same dashboards, from more than 60 sources.
  • You want reports that arrive on their own. Scheduled emails with a PDF, and dashboards clients can open any day.
  • You're a larger team. Many seats, a dedicated success manager, and on Prime, single sign-on and sending data on to BigQuery or Looker Studio, some of them as add-ons.

Where Roster is the better choice

  • You run Google Ads and Meta for a handful of clients. A reporting platform priced for large agencies costs more than reporting is worth to you.
  • You already work in Claude. Reports and campaigns are done where you're already working, by asking.
  • You set campaigns up as well as report on them. Roster drafts new Google Ads and Meta campaigns with you, and creates them paused only after your yes.
  • You want to start today. No demo, no sales call, no card.

Questions

How much does Whatagraph cost?
On 2 November 2026, whatagraph.com listed the Max plan from €699 a month, billed yearly, and the Prime plan at a custom price, both bought through a demo. Check their pricing page for today's figures.
Is there a free alternative to Whatagraph for Google Ads and Meta reports?
Roster is free during early access for Google Ads and Meta Ads reports and campaign setup. Looker Studio is free too, but you build and maintain each report yourself; see Roster vs Looker Studio.
Can I use both?
Yes. Some agencies keep a platform for multi-channel dashboards and use Roster for Google Ads and Meta campaign setup and monthly client reports.
