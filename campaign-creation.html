Campaign creation

Your test, built for you.

You decide what to test and why. Roster does the building: it drafts each campaign from your brief, then shows you every part of it before anything is created.

Every variant, from one brief

Budget, bidding, locations and languages, ad groups or ad sets, and every ad, drafted from your brief, once for each version you want to test.

Checked before you see it

Roster checks the draft against its own rules, then Google or Meta checks it too, without creating anything.

Created paused

How it works.

In Roster and the Claude desktop app, with the Roster plugin, once campaign creation is on for your agency. The setup guide walks you through it.

  1. Start it in Roster

    Pick the client's ad account, and Roster checks what it can set up there. Describe the campaign in a line, press Copy for Claude and paste it into Claude.

  2. Review the draft

    Claude asks what's missing, then shows you the full campaign and every ad. Ask for changes until it's right; the campaign's page in Roster keeps the latest draft.

  3. Say yes

    Roster creates it in the client's account, paused. Switch it on when you're ready.

Questions.

Can Roster change campaigns that are already running?

No. It only creates new campaigns, and only after your yes. It never edits, pauses or removes anything already in the account.

Which campaign types can it create?

In Meta, now in early access: traffic, sales and leads campaigns for Facebook and Instagram. In Google Ads, when it opens soon: Search, Display, Performance Max and Demand Gen.

Do I need to know every setting?

No. You decide what the campaign should do and approve the draft. Roster handles the settings, and Google or Meta checks the campaign before it's created, so a problem shows up before anything is live.
