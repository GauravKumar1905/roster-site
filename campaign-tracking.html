How it works.

In the Claude desktop app, with the Roster plugin. The setup guide walks you through it.

  1. Pick what to watch

    Tell Claude which campaigns matter and what good looks like: the budget, and the cost per result you're aiming for.

  2. Roster keeps watch

    Pacing and every ad's numbers are checked while the campaign runs.

  3. You get the short list

    What to change or pause, and why. Nothing changes until you say so.

Questions.

Does Roster pause ads by itself?

No. It tells you which ads to pause and why. Nothing changes in the account until you say so.

Which platforms does it watch?

Google Ads and Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram).

Does it cost extra?

No. Like every part of Roster, it's free during early access.
