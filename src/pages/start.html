Getting started

Everything happens in Roster’s dashboard, with nothing to install. You need a Roster login and the Google or Facebook login you already use for your clients’ ads. Reports work today; Meta Ads campaign creation is in early access, and Google Ads campaign creation opens soon.

  1. Create your login

    Create one login for your agency. Roster opens on Home and asks what you want to do first. You can pick a report straight away, and Roster asks for each sign-in as it needs it; the steps below take the same path one piece at a time.

    Create account

  2. Connect your ad accounts, once

    In Settings, under Connections, press Connect Google Ads or Set up Meta Ads, and sign in with the Google or Facebook login that manages your clients’ ads. You do this once, for your agency, not for each client.

    If you also report on a client’s website, Connect Search Console and Connect Google Analytics are in the same place.

    Meta Ads, during early access: Meta asks us to invite you as a tester before you can connect. Register as a Meta developer (it’s free). Then, in Settings under Set up Meta Ads, give the Facebook profile link or name you’ll connect with and press Ask for an invite. Accept the invite at developers.facebook.com/requests once it arrives, then connect.

  3. Add a client

    In Clients, press Add client. Name it and tick the accounts that are theirs. The client’s reports, tracking and campaigns all start from there.

  4. Build your first report

    Press New report and pick the kind: Google Ads, Meta Ads, Search Console or Google Analytics. Choose the client’s account, add anything Roster should know, and press Build it.

    Roster builds it with you on the report’s page, one page at a time, from live figures. It shows each page before saving it, asks when it needs a choice from you, and goes on when you say so. You can leave and come back: Home lists anything waiting for you under Needs you.

    When it’s ready, check the figures and add your note. Then press Share with client, and Create link: Roster copies the link, ready to paste into your email.

    Campaign creation is in early access. To turn it on for your agency, email gauravkr.contact@gmail.com. Once it’s on, a Meta Ads campaign starts the same way: press New campaign, choose the client’s ad account, describe the campaign, and press Draft it. Roster drafts it with you on the campaign’s page, step by step, and shows you every ad first. Roster creates the campaign paused, only after your yes. Google Ads campaigns open soon.

  5. Know where tracking is

    In Tracking, press Track campaigns, pick a client’s ad account and tick the campaigns to watch. Each morning Roster checks them and lists what needs you. It only reads: ask Roster about any item if you want it explained.
