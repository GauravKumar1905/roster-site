How it works

Up and running in three steps.

  1. 01

    Pick a report

    Sign up and pick Google Ads report or Meta Ads report. Sign in once with the Google or Facebook login that manages your clients' ads, then choose the client's ad account.

  2. 02

    Build it with Claude

    Press Copy for Claude and paste it into the Claude desktop app. Claude shows you each page of a report, or each ad of a campaign (Meta Ads in early access, Google Ads soon), before anything is saved.

  3. 03

    Send it, or launch it

    Send the client one link to the report. Approve a campaign and it's created paused, so nothing spends until you switch it on.

Questions

Before you sign up.

Anything else? Write to us and you'll hear back from a person.

Does Roster change anything in my ad accounts?

Only when you ask it to set up a campaign. It shows you the campaign and every ad first, creates it only after you say yes, and creates it paused, so nothing runs or spends until you switch it on. It never edits, pauses or removes anything already in the account.

Does Roster run my campaigns for me?

No, on purpose. How you run a campaign is your call: it depends on the client, the market and what you've learned. Roster does the work around it, building the reports and the campaigns you ask for, and leaves every decision to you.

Which ad platforms does it cover?

Google Ads and Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram). Client reports work in both today. Campaign creation opens soon for Google Ads, and is in early access for Meta Ads: to turn it on for your agency, email gauravkr.contact@gmail.com.

Which Claude do I need?

The Claude desktop app, with Cowork or Claude Code. That's where the Roster plugin runs. The setup guide shows how to add it.

What do I need to get started?

A Roster account, the Google or Facebook login you already use for your clients' ads, and the Claude desktop app with the Roster plugin. The setup guide walks through it in three steps.

Do my clients need an account?

No. They open the link you send in any browser, on any device.

Can one client see another client's data?

No. Each client's accounts and reports are kept apart, and a link shows only the report it was made for.

Why does Meta need an invite?

While Roster is in early access, Meta asks us to add each agency as a tester before its ad accounts can connect. We send the invite; you accept it once.

How much does it cost?

Nothing during early access. When pricing is introduced, early users will hear about it first, well before anything changes.

Where can I read how my data is handled?

On the security page, in plain words, and in full in the privacy policy.
