How it works.

In Roster and the Claude desktop app, with the Roster plugin. The setup guide walks you through it.

  1. Start it in Roster

    Pick the report and the client's ad account, and press Copy for Claude. Claude proposes Roster's standard monthly report and builds it with you, page by page, with real figures.

  2. Refresh it each month

    When the month closes, refresh the report from live data and add your note.

  3. Send one link

    Your client opens it in any browser, on any device. No login, no PDF.

Questions.

Can I use the report format I already send?

Yes. Share it — a spreadsheet, slides or a PDF — and Roster turns it into a template you can use for every client.

Does the report update itself?

No, on purpose. You refresh it when you're ready, and each link you send keeps the figures it was sent with.

Do my clients need an account?

No. They open the link you send in any browser, on any device.
