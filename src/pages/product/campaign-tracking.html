How it works.

On your Roster dashboard, with nothing to install. The setup guide walks you through it.

  1. Pick the campaigns

    Choose a client's ad account and tick the campaigns to track, any campaign, not only ones Roster set up. Add the budget and the cost per result you're aiming for, if you have them.

  2. Roster checks each morning

    Early in the account's time zone, weekends too. Check now looks again whenever you like.

  3. You get the short list

    On your dashboard, where you can ask Roster about any item. Nothing changes in the account.

Questions.

Does Roster pause ads by itself?

No. Tracking only reads. Each item says what to do, and you make the change in Google Ads or Ads Manager. Ask Roster about an item if you want it explained.

Can it track campaigns Roster didn't set up?

Yes. Any campaign in a client's connected Google Ads or Meta account. You pick which ones; it never watches a whole account.

Does it email me?

Not yet. The list is on your Roster dashboard.

Which platforms does it watch?

Google Ads and Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram).

Does it cost extra?

No. Like every part of Roster, it's free during early access.
