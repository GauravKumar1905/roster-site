1. What Roster does

Roster stores report definitions and renders them against live Google Ads data. You connect a Google account per client workspace, choose which advertising accounts and campaigns a report covers, and Roster produces a dashboard you can share.

Roster is read-only with respect to your advertising accounts. It does not create, modify, pause or delete campaigns, budgets, bids, keywords or creatives, and it is not an advertising management tool.
